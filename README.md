## About Me

- AI researcher focused on world models and visual generative learning.
- Interested in video generation, immersive scene generation, and 3D computer vision.
- Advised by Prof. Yunchao Wei and Prof. Xiaojie Jin at Beijing Jiaotong University.
- Writing research notes, technical essays, and project updates on [Nayuta](https://ke-xing.github.io/Nayuta/).
- Open to research conversations, collaboration, and thoughtful technical exchange.

## Research Highlights

- **StereoWorld**. Geometry-Aware Monocular-to-Stereo Video Generation. `CVPR 2026`. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09363) | [Project](https://ke-xing.github.io/StereoWorld/)
- **TiP4GEN**. Text to Immersive Panorama 4D Scene Generation. `ACM Multimedia 2025`. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12415) | [Project](https://ke-xing.github.io/TiP4GEN/)
- **STDA**. Beyond Wide-Angle Images: Unsupervised Video Portrait Correction via Spatiotemporal Diffusion Adaptation. `AAAI 2026`. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00401)
